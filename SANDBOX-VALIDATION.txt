Conglobate sandbox: pre-implementation validation ledger
=====================================================

Trust model: VM kernel, supervisor, and filesystem worker are trusted. Recipe
commands are untrusted, including namespace root. This ledger concerns a
prototype; it does not certify the existing production executor. That executor
still uses initial-namespace root and exposes context and must be redesigned.

Status meanings:
- Evidence: specific behavior reproduced, bounded by the stated experiment.
- Limitation: observed behavior that constrains the supported build contract.
- Blocker: must be resolved before selecting/implementing this design.
- Pending: no adequate validation yet; not a pass.

1. Identity and capabilities — candidate restriction evidence / pending qualification
   Mapped root records target IDs on disk. UID 65535 succeeds; UID 65536 is
   rejected with EINVAL. Initial root is not granted to commands. The final
   capability bounding set, no_new_privs and syscall restrictions have now been
   exercised in validation/sandbox/policy, including RPM reinstall/remove/install.
   Dropping userns SYS_ADMIN breaks SELinux xattr writes; retaining it while
   filtering mount/namespace syscalls restores them. This is a candidate policy,
   not a completed interface audit or a security-complete verdict.

2. Process isolation — partial evidence
   Outer worker PID cannot be signaled, traced or opened by pidfd. Fresh proc
   mount does not expose it. Detached double-fork/setsid processes die with
   PID-namespace init. Remaining: nested namespaces, process_vm, namespace-FD
   acquisition and tests under the final restrictions.
   Candidate-policy null-argument process_vm/ptrace/pidfd_getfd calls return
   EPERM; namespace-creating clone/unshare/setns are denied. Ordinary fork/thread
   and detached-descendant cleanup pass. NS_GET_PARENT on own userns is denied.

   This does not yet cover all loader/environment or final restriction choices.
