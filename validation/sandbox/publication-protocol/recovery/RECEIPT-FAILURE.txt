Receipt write failure controls — 2026-10-21
==========================================
Diagnostic extension of chain-recovery-records. Two cases use the actual completed
FUSE build artifact and normal safe-Rust publication. Existing thirteen cases are
retained. Production machine formats remain CBOR; these JSON records are fixtures.

After a successful Committed reply, a separate trusted fixture child attempts
record() with a real valid receipt. Only that child sets RLIMIT_FSIZE=16 and
ignores SIGXFSZ. The write/flush encounters actual EFBIG and leaves a sixteen-byte
partial receipt.tmp. The diagnostic catches only EFBIG; another errno or success
fails the case. No fault is injected into the filesystem or supervisor process.

receipt-write-failure:
There was no previous receipt. The failed temporary write cannot install one.
The public artifact's file hashes remain identical, the publisher is killed and
reaped, and a fresh reader reports VisibleAcknowledgmentUnknown. A partial temp
record is not evidence of success and its contents are not parsed by recovery.

receipt-replacement-failure:
A complete valid receipt already exists before the limited replacement attempt.
The same EFBIG leaves the prior receipt byte-identical. After publisher death,
the fresh reader reports Committed using the old final record; the partial temp
record neither invalidates it nor becomes its replacement.

Both cases preserve output and partial temporary data until harness assertions
finish. Recovery performs no retry or deletion. The stable prepared record,
parent protection and stopped-writer assumptions from RECORDS.txt still apply.
The existing record() creates its temp file exclusively; a leftover temp requires
an explicit recovery policy rather than blind repeated writes. That policy and
orphan cleanup are not implemented here. This test covers write failure before
atomic replacement, not record fsync/replace/parent-sync failure or process death
inside those operations. EFBIG is not a simulated media/writeback EIO or ENOSPC.
