Publication syscall-error controls — 2026-10-21
===============================================
Diagnostic extension of chain-recovery-records; no production change.

The three new cases exercise actual syscall failures in commit-arbiter, without
simulated EIO or damaged storage. All ten previous record cases run unchanged.

rename-failure:
After reservation, the trusted harness moves owned staging to a holding name.
The real renameat2 cannot find its source and returns an error. The coordinator
reports Failed and output is absent. Restoring staging does not implicitly retry:
subsequent commit/cancel both report Failed. After process death, a fresh reader
can establish only PreparedNotVisible from its prepared record and intact staging;
there is no terminal failure receipt in this case. It must not infer permission
to restart or garbage-collect from that classification.

sync-error-unrecorded and sync-error-receipt:
An explicit diagnostic --path-parent option opens the parent with O_PATH rather
than a normal directory FD. That descriptor works as renameat2's directory anchor
but fsync rejects it with EBADF; startup checks that exact error. The real rename
therefore succeeds and the real sync_all fails. The coordinator reports
VisibleError; repeated status/cancel/commit preserve that terminal outcome and
the complete output. No cancellation acceptance, implicit retry or deletion.

The fresh recovery process reports VisibleAcknowledgmentUnknown without a
receipt, or VisibleError when the trusted recorder has stored a receipt for that
phase referencing the prepared-record digest. The latter is evidence of the
recorded error, not evidence that the output is absent or corrupt. A Committed
receipt is still required for the diagnostic Committed classification.

Limits:
O_PATH intentionally supplies an unsuitable synchronization FD. This validates
the post-rename error branch against a real failing syscall; it is NOT a storage
writeback EIO, ENOSPC, torn-media or power-loss experiment. Ordinary tmpfs remains
the environment contract. No receipt write/sync failure or concurrent recovery
admission is tested by these cases. The earlier protected-parent, stable identity,
quiescent-writer and diagnostic JSON-format caveats still apply.
