Remaining validation dependencies after the workload/control follow-ups
======================================================================
Scope reconciliation, not a completion verdict. The21 numbered requirements in
SANDBOX-VALIDATION.txt remain authoritative, including its explicit separation
of prototype validation from after-implementation executor reruns.

Recent gates actually closed in their stated scope
--------------------------------------------------
Paired runtime mount preflight and ordinary setup-error teardown; command-local
anonymous descriptors; single-scute and restricted-derived two-scute corium root
boots; repository-solved Fedora43->44 distribution transaction and kernel remount;
live coordinator socket exclusion during success and accepted cancellation.
Their sources/logs and limitations are indexed; none resolves backend semantics.

Unresolved architecture choices and their dependent tests
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Required filesystem semantics (area11): the mapped-FUSE boundary cannot
   provide the tested trusted.* attributes, immutable/append changes and device
   operations. Further successful package runs cannot establish those missing
   semantics. A different boundary must preserve them. UML remains a proposed
   investigation, not an accepted architecture or an executed prototype. Do not
   silently waive these requirements or deploy an alternative to close a box.
   Once a candidate is chosen: repeat the failing semantic matrix first, then
   port the restriction/process/metadata/workload/lifecycle tests it affects.
2. Identity ownership (8/20): no reserved pool/allocation/reuse protocol is
   adopted. Fixed numeric ranges in fixtures are not an allocator. Select the
   environment-provided pool and coordinator ownership rule, then validate
   concurrent assignment, retained resources, complete release and interrupted
   registration/recovery. Key residue is not a bypass of the tested key-API deny.
3. Coordinator/recovery selection (14–18): passing normal/cancel/death/OOM
   prototypes do not choose a final composition. Keep authoritative lifetime
   handles; an unlocked lease or reused numeric PID is insufficient. Once the
   composition is selected, enumerate its concrete transitions and test those;
   do not keep inventing unnamed phase combinations or promise kernel I/O bounds.
4. Runtime/profile adoption (1–4/7/19/21): qualification must name actual worker,
   loader/config/device/descriptor ownership and maintenance. The existing
   binary/appliance contract already assigns startup trust and future full-
   runtime attestation to the environment. A new standalone self-measurement
   inventory schema is NOT an unresolved requirement. Child runtime protection
   and real recipe/inventory/control integration remain binary obligations.

- A real VPN-only hostname is still needed from the user for that requested

Deferred rather than silently missing
------------------------------------
The original checklist explicitly requires after-implementation reruns against
the real executor. Prototype evidence cannot satisfy those reruns in advance.
Hardware attestation and appliance transport are explicitly deferred. Fully
rootless assembly is conditional on selection; it is not a new mandatory target.
Booting the upgraded output, arbitrary layer depth, all-service health and every
possible kernel syscall are not new requirements introduced by the recent tests.
Their absence limits those claims; it does not justify endless replacement gates.

The full goal remains incomplete. These distinctions preserve its scope and
identify where executable tests can make progress versus where an architecture
choice is necessary. The named independent recovery, collector and admission
follow-ups above are now recorded as completed within their stated scopes.
They are not remaining work that can substitute for the backend decision.
Next decision: investigate a bounded UML prototype, or compare other filesystem
isolation boundaries first. Neither choice constitutes adoption. The actual
VPN-only DNS check separately awaits a hostname. Final-executor reruns remain
after-implementation work, not runnable prototype checks being deferred here.
